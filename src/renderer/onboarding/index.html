Welcome to Bitbot

Bitbot is a little creature that lives on your screen and gets fed when you use your computer.

Your privacy

Bitbot counts how many keys you press and clicks you make. It never sees what you type, never reads your windows, and never connects to the internet. Everything stays on this Mac.

Let Bitbot count keys and clicks

Bitbot gets fed when you type and click, so it needs to count your key presses and clicks. macOS calls this Input Monitoring. Bitbot only counts: it never sees what you type.

Turned it on? macOS may need Bitbot to restart before it takes effect.

You can skip this: Bitbot still eats when you move the mouse, open apps and take breaks. You can turn it on later from the menu bar.
